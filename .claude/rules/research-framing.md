# Framing external work against your project

Loads when you read a source, brainstorm, comparison, dispatch or concept. Applies to "why this matters for us" lines, comparison intros and subagent prompts that summarize external work.

Do not write a confident one-liner. Short framings match on shared vocabulary (entities, memory, routing, agents) and round up to "validates our architecture". Force three clauses:

1. **Surface similarity.** What shared language makes the comparison tempting?
2. **Substrate difference.** Same words, different mechanism? Check weights vs corpus, training vs inference, model vs operator, internal vs external routing. If one flips, the framing flips.
3. **Actual bearing.** After 1 and 2, what claim is left? "Adds a citation", "influences positioning" are fine. If thin, write "no direct bearing; landscape only".

Ground your project's side in `STATE.md` and the newest synthesis, not in a remembered description: products get reframed, and older brainstorms may describe a different one. Full protocol: `AGENT-GUIDE.md` § "Comparison protocol".
