# Drafting: anything the team will send, submit or publish

Always loaded. Applies to emails, replies, DMs, application answers, bios, scripts, posts, briefs and decks. If the optional voice plugin is installed, `/write-as` drafts in a team member's voice; the rules below hold either way. Add your team's own style rules at the bottom.

## Facts

- Every statement about us comes from `STATE.md` or a KB file opened this session. Otherwise write a `[placeholder]` and list it after the draft. Never invent a customer name, a number, a date or an acronym expansion.
- Numbers: the newest `traction/` file. One strongest true metric beats five; name the evidence type when it matters (measured, customer-written, verbal).
- Full canonical names on every mention; check `ALIASES.md` and `GLOSSARY.md` § "Named entities — canonical names".
- Respect the never-say list in `STATE.md`.

## Audience

- Open the recipient's hub and the last interaction before drafting. Do not explain to people what they already know, were present for, or told us.
- Report final facts only. No drafting history or "we first thought X, then found Y" in anything a judge, investor or customer reads.
- External readers do not know internal vocabulary. No references to our own notes or logs; no term defined only in this KB. State the fact in plain words.
- One scope per document: do not pull in material from outside what the document is about.

## Shape and scope

- Short by default. If the user supplies a draft, keep their structure, opening and wording and change only what they asked for.
- Draft only. Do not send, submit, edit the live document, create todos or write to the KB unless asked.
- When the user says a draft was sent or submitted, offer one line to stamp it on the recipient's hub.
